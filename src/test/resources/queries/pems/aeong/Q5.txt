// AeonG (transaction time): the segment of a station is its inSegment edge, valid while it exists (see PemsAeonG); TT: the transaction times of the times of PemsWindows, recorded by the ingestion
MATCH (g:Segment)<-[:inSegment]-(s:Station) TT AS {TTT} WITH DISTINCT s MATCH (s)-[:hasTimeSeries]->(:TimeSeries)-[:hasMeasurement]->(m:lane0_avg_speed) TT FROM 0 TO {TTLAST} RETURN s.station_id, avg(m.value);
