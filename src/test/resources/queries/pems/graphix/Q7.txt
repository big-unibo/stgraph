FROM GRAPH Pems MATCH (g:`Segment`)<-[:`inSegment`]-(v:`StationVersion`)<-[:`hasVersion`]-(s:`Station`)-[:`hasTimeSeries`]->(t:`TimeSeries`)-[:`hasMeasurement`]->(m:`Measurement`) WHERE m.`label` = "lane0_avg_speed" AND v.`valid_from` <= m.`timestamp` AND m.`timestamp` < v.`valid_to` AND {FROM} <= m.`timestamp` AND m.`timestamp` < {TO} GROUP BY g.`segment_id` AS seg SELECT seg, AVG(m.`value`) AS avgValue;
