SmartBench evaluation: inconsistencies to examine
=================================================


1. Queries
----------

General


EnvironmentOutlier



- Graphix: wrong result because of an engine bug, FIXED at load time. In AsterixDB 0.9.9 (both the one of STGraph and
  the one of Graphix), st_geom_from_text returns, for every row of a query, the geometry of the first row
  (SELECT VALUE st_as_text(st_geom_from_text(s)) FROM ["LINESTRING(34 0, 63 20)", "LINESTRING(4 15, 9 19)"] AS s
  returns the first line twice); so do st_geom_from_geojson and st_geom_from_wkb, also on a field, in a LET or in a
  subquery. Constants work, and so does st_make_point. STGraph is not affected: it sends one query per series, with
  the location of the environment as a constant WKT (AsterixSyntaxParser). The Graphix query converted the location
  of every environment in the same query, so every environment got the line of the first one: on small 1 or 0
  environments above the threshold instead of 4, depending on the plan. Now the loader of Graphix stores the WKT
  locations of the entities (the 340 infrastructures on small) as geometries (2D, as the events), each written as a
  constant in the INSERT of its batch (not through a data feed), and the query intersects them directly: on small,
  the 4 environments of STGraph, with the averages of one query per environment with its constant WKT. Difference
  from STGraph left: Graphix stores the location of an entity as a geometry, STGraph as a string (converted in each
  query).

- Graphix, model: FIXED. The graph had one Temperature vertex per event and an edge from each sensor to each of its
  events (through a join of TimeSeries and Observations). Now it has the model of STGraph (hasTemperature, then
  HasTS): sensor -[hasTemperature]-> time series (Temperature, a record of TimeSeries) -[hasTS]-> events
  (TemperatureEvent, the records of Observations, whose tsid points to their series). The queries are graph patterns
  down to the events, as before; EnvironmentCoverage stops at the series, as in STGraph.

- Graphix, events read twice: FIXED with a setting of Graphix (graphix.evaluation.minimize-joins=true in its
  cc.conf, default false; it belongs to the Graphix extension, the AsterixDB of STGraph does not have it). Graphix
  evaluates a pattern as joins between the views of its vertices and edges: by default every query scanned all the
  events twice (the edge hasTS and the vertex TemperatureEvent, the same records) and joined them on (tsid, _id);
  on large (259,200,000 events) EnvironmentCoverage did not end in two days, on 1 core. With minimize-joins, an
  event is read once. On small, same results as before, row by row (EnvironmentOutlier: see above).

- Graphix, primary key of the events: FIXED with a setting of Graphix (graphix.evaluation.prefer-indexnl=true in its
  cc.conf, default false; it belongs to the Graphix extension, the AsterixDB of STGraph does not have it), with a
  trade-off. Without it, the queries read the events with a scan of Observations (hash joins), not through the
  primary key (tsid, _id) as STGraph does: e.g. AgentHistory, one sensor, scanned all the events. The optimizer of
  Graphix uses an index in a join only with a hint (/*+ indexnl */), which the queries do not have, and it has no
  cost-based optimizer. Its documentation says that Graphix uses the CBO of AsterixDB, but the optimizer of its last
  release (0.4.1-alpha, extension 0.2.0-SNAPSHOT) does not: its rule set (GraphixRuleSetFactory) rebuilds the rule
  collections of AsterixDB without the CBO one (buildCBORuleCollection, with EnumerateJoinsRule, in the
  DefaultRuleSetFactory of AsterixDB), for every query, also plain SQL++. The plan is the same after ANALYZE DATASET,
  also with SET `compiler.cbo` "true", while on the AsterixDB 0.9.9 of STGraph, after ANALYZE, the same join uses the
  primary key by itself. prefer-indexnl places the hint on every join between vertices and edges, whatever its
  cost: it reads the events of a series through (tsid) and pays off for selective navigation (AgentHistory, on
  small 0.18 s instead of 2.5 s, on medium 0.25 s instead of 8.9 s), but it also turns the joins that follow the
  events into one index search per event (e.g., hasOwner of MaintenanceOwners, a search on Sensors per event): on
  small and medium EnvironmentAggregate and MaintenanceOwners take about 50% more, EnvironmentOutlier and
  AgentOutlier 5-10% more (averages of 9 iterations, with minimize-joins in both cases). Alone, without
  minimize-joins, it also looks every event up again as a vertex (on small, AgentOutlier 20 s). Difference from
  STGraph left: the time window is applied after reading the events of a series, since the tsid comes from a join
  (STGraph searches (tsid, _id) in the window).


2. Loaders (Kotlin port vs Python code)
---------------------------------------

Timing


  One difference left, forced by the engine: the AsterixDB of Graphix (0.9.9-SNAPSHOT) stores the last, partial
  frame of a feed only when the feed stops, while the 0.9.9 of STGraph stores it with the feed open (checked on
  small: 2,591,904 of 2,592,000 events readable before STOP FEED, all of them after; same client, 1,000 events:
  1,000 readable with the socket open on the 0.9.9, 896 on the AsterixDB of Graphix). So each Graphix worker closes
  its feed (as STGraph closes its feeds) before waiting for its last event, once AsterixDB has read its whole stream
  (half-closed socket, AsterixDB closes its side at the end of the stream: stopping a feed that is still reading
  loses the unread events). The wait until the stream is read is time-series time, as the rest of the wait; the
  close (STOP FEED) is index time, as in STGraph.
  Graphix cannot run on the AsterixDB 0.9.9 of STGraph: it needs Hyracks and Algebricks changes that never reached
  a release (graphix-asterixdb/mirror), and its jar does not load on the 0.9.9 (NoSuchMethodError).
  The Graphix ingestions in results/ before this change are not comparable with STGraph.

PGAge
- String values are not escaped: an apostrophe in a value makes the whole entity fail (it is logged and skipped).
- Batch insert: one INSERT of 20,000 rows (execute_values) in Python, JDBC batches rewritten into multi-row INSERTs
  (reWriteBatchedInserts) here.
- Not ported: multi-device entities and GeoJSON locations (SmartBench has neither).

Neo4j

- The events have a range index on Temperature.timestamp, the temporal index of the other systems, built after the
  load (index time); the Python code did not create it. No index on the ids of the entities (e.g., Sensor.id, used
  by every UNWIND batch and by the MATCH (v {id: ...}) of the dumps), as PGAge has none on its graph.

- The dumps are read from datasets/original/smartbench/neo4j/<size> (./downloadDataset.sh smartbench/neo4j <size>);
  their names may follow the old sizes (see below).


3. Configuration and infrastructure
-----------------------------------
- Sizes: the old time_constraints_tstamp.yaml used small/large/big/big_long; time_constraints.yaml of STGraph has the
  same windows as small/medium/large (large -> medium, big -> large), plus xsmall and extralarge. FIXED: the
  evaluation runs AeonG and HyGraph with the old names (small -> small, medium -> large, large -> big).
- Neo4j: the old image was FROM neo4j (latest at build time); the compose now pins neo4j:5.26 (conf with server.*
  keys, so 5.x). OPEN: check the version used for the paper.
- PGAge: the Dockerfile clones apache/age master (not pinned). OPEN.
- PGAge: conf/postgresql.conf preloads only timescaledb (AGE is loaded per session). The original evaluation set
  shared_buffers = 64 GB and effective_cache_size = 193 GB. FIXED: they are the defaults of PostgreSQL now.
- Memory: Neo4j has a page cache of 512 MB (conf/neo4j.conf), the other systems their defaults. OPEN.
- AeonG and HyGraph run their client inside their container, limited to one core: client and database share it,
  while the client of STGraph, Neo4j, PGAge and Graphix (the evaluation) runs outside the container of the database.
  OPEN.
- HyGraph compose: chmod +x scripts/run_evaluation.sh.sh (typo); scripts/run_evaluation.sh runs
  python3 evaluate_hygraph.py from /hygraph, while the file is in src/smartbench/. OPEN.
