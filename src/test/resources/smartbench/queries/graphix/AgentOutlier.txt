FROM GRAPH SmartBench
MATCH (i:`Infrastructure`)<-[:`hasCoverage`]-(s:`Sensor`)-[:`hasTemperature`]->(ts:`Temperature`)-[:`hasTS`]->(m:`TemperatureEvent`)
WHERE m.`_id` >= {TSTAMPFROM_PARAM} AND m.`_id` < {TSTAMPTO_PARAM}
GROUP BY s.id AS device, i.id AS environment
